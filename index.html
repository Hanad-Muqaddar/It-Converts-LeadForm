ARTICLE III. Terms & Conditions
INITIAL ORDER & PAYMENT TERMS

§ 3.1 — INITIAL CALL ORDER

Buyer agrees to purchase an initial test batch of ten (10) qualified inbound Final Expense calls.

§ 3.2 — CALL PRICING

Buyer shall pay USD $40.00 per qualified inbound call, for a total initial order value of USD $400.00.

§ 3.3 — QUALIFICATION CRITERIA

A call shall be deemed qualified and billable if:

  • The call is related to Final Expense insurance.
  • The call duration is at least ninety (90) seconds.
  • The call is not fraudulent, automated, duplicate, or otherwise invalid.

§ 3.4 — PREPAYMENT REQUIREMENT

The full payment of USD $400.00 must be received prior to the delivery of any calls. Call delivery will begin only after payment has been confirmed.

§ 3.5 — DELIVERY

Supplier will make commercially reasonable efforts to deliver the ten (10) qualified calls within the agreed campaign period. Delivery timelines are estimates and not guarantees.

§ 3.6 — DISPUTES

Any dispute regarding call qualification must be submitted in writing within five (5) business days of call delivery. Calls not disputed within this period shall be deemed accepted.

§ 3.7 — NO GUARANTEE OF SALES

Supplier does not guarantee appointments, policy sales, issued policies, revenue, or conversion rates. Buyer acknowledges that payment is based solely on the delivery of qualified calls.

In the event that fewer than ten (10) qualified calls are delivered, the remaining prepaid balance shall be applied toward future call deliveries unless otherwise agreed by both parties in writing.
